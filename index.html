A Highway 6 kitchen, run like a family table.

Restaurant Los Agaves opened in Providence Plaza with one idea: cook the way our own families cooked at home, and don't cut corners to do it faster. The comal stays hot from 8 in the morning until the last table on a Friday night.

Every fajita plate leaves the kitchen still sizzling. Every batch of salsa is made in-house. And the molcajete — the volcanic stone bowl our Molcajeteada Mixta is served in — comes straight from the same tradition as the agave plants that give the restaurant its name.
